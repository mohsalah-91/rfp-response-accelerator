# Data Protection Policy Summary

Maintained by Legal and Privacy. Summary of the internal policy, approved for external distribution.

## Scope

This policy covers all customer content processed by the Halebrook platform, all personal data of customer personnel held for account administration, and all data held by subprocessors.

## Classification

Data is classified in four tiers. Public covers published material. Internal covers operational data with no customer content. Confidential covers customer content, contracts, and security findings. Restricted covers credentials, encryption keys, and audit evidence.

Handling requirements escalate by tier. Restricted data may not be stored outside designated systems and may not be transmitted over email under any circumstances.

## Access control

Access follows least privilege with mandatory quarterly recertification. Access to systems holding Confidential or Restricted data requires hardware-backed multi-factor authentication and is available only from managed devices.

Standing access to customer content is not granted to any role. Access is obtained through the time-bound elevation process described in the security architecture documentation.

## Personnel

All personnel complete security and privacy training at onboarding and annually thereafter. Background checks are performed where permitted by local law. All personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations surviving termination.

Access is revoked within one hour of termination, and the revocation process is tested quarterly.

## Incident response

Security incidents are triaged against a published severity matrix. Where an incident involves customer content, the affected customer is notified without undue delay and within 48 hours of confirmation, regardless of contractual notice periods.

Notification includes what is known, what is not yet known, and what action the customer should consider. A written post-incident report follows within 15 business days.

## Breach notification under GDPR

Where Halebrook becomes aware of a personal data breach, the controller is notified without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours, to allow the controller to meet its own 72-hour obligation.

## Secure development

All code changes require peer review. Static analysis and dependency scanning run on every change. Penetration testing is performed annually by an independent firm, and a summary report is available under NDA.

Findings are tracked to remediation against severity-based timelines defined in the vulnerability management policy.
