# Subprocessors

Maintained by Legal. Current as of October 2026. The authoritative list is published at halebrook.example/subprocessors.

## Infrastructure

Amazon Web Services provides compute, storage, and networking for US East, US West, EU West, and APAC Southeast. Data processed: all customer content in those regions.

Microsoft Azure provides compute, storage, and networking for EU Central and APAC South. Data processed: all customer content in those regions.

Both are engaged under their standard data processing terms with Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

## Platform services

Cloudflare provides edge termination and denial of service protection in all regions. Data processed: connection metadata, no customer content at rest.

Twilio provides transactional SMS for multi-factor authentication where a customer has enabled SMS as a factor. Data processed: phone number and one-time code. US only.

SendGrid provides transactional email. Data processed: recipient address and message content for notifications. US only.

## Business systems

Zendesk provides the support case system. Data processed: support case content, which may contain customer-supplied diagnostic data. Customers on Enterprise can request region-restricted case handling.

Atlassian provides internal engineering issue tracking. Customer content is not permitted in engineering issues; diagnostic data is referenced by case identifier only.

## Notification of changes

Halebrook notifies customers at least 30 days before adding or replacing a subprocessor that will process customer content. Notification is through the administrator channel and the published subprocessor page, which offers subscription.

A customer may object to a new subprocessor within the notice period. Where an objection cannot be resolved, the customer may terminate the affected service without penalty for the remainder of the term.

## Subprocessor assurance

Each subprocessor is assessed before engagement and reassessed annually. Assessment covers certification status, data residency, breach history, and contractual terms.

Assessment records are available to customers under NDA.
