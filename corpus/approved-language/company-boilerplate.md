# Approved Company Language

Maintained by Marketing and Legal. This is the only approved wording for company description, scale claims, and customer references. Do not paraphrase the claims in the first three sections.

## Company description

Halebrook Systems builds infrastructure and security software for organizations that need to see and control what is on their network. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Austin, Texas, Halebrook employs approximately 400 people across offices in Austin, Dublin, and Singapore.

## Scale claims, approved

More than 600 organizations use Halebrook products.

Halebrook monitors more than 11 million assets across its customer base.

The largest single deployment monitors approximately 180,000 assets.

Do not state a customer count above 600, an asset count above 11 million, or a revenue figure of any kind. Revenue is not disclosed.

## Customer references

No customer may be named without written permission recorded in the reference register. The register is maintained by Customer Marketing.

Three customers currently permit naming in RFP responses. Contact Customer Marketing for the current list before naming anyone; permissions are revoked as often as they are granted.

Anonymized references are permitted in the form "a North American healthcare system with approximately 40,000 assets." Do not include enough detail to identify the organization.

## Claims that must not be made

Do not claim any certification not listed in the certifications document.

Do not claim that the platform prevents breaches. Approved phrasing is that the platform detects and enables response to malicious activity.

Do not claim 100% detection coverage, zero false positives, or any specific detection rate. Detection efficacy varies by environment and no benchmark figure is approved for external use.

Do not commit to a roadmap date. Approved phrasing for roadmap items is that the capability is under development with no committed date.

Do not compare Halebrook to a named competitor in writing.

## Pricing

Pricing is never included in a technical RFP response. Where an RFP requests pricing in the technical section, respond that commercial terms are provided separately and direct the reader to the commercial response.
