# Support and Escalation

Responses drawn from the Northgate Logistics RFP (2026) and the Meridian Health Network RFP (2025). Approved by Customer Support.

## What support tiers are available?

Three tiers. Standard provides business-hours support in the customer's home region. Premium provides 24x7 coverage. Elite provides 24x7 coverage plus a named Technical Account Manager and quarterly service reviews.

Standard is included with every subscription. Premium and Elite are priced separately.

## What are your response time targets?

Severity 1, defined as a complete loss of service or a confirmed active security incident: 30 minutes on Premium and Elite, 4 business hours on Standard.

Severity 2, significant degradation with no workaround: 2 hours on Premium and Elite, 8 business hours on Standard.

Severity 3, degradation with a workaround available: 8 business hours on all tiers.

Severity 4, questions and feature requests: 2 business days on all tiers.

These are response targets, not resolution targets. Resolution commitments are not offered because resolution time depends on the nature of the fault.

## How do we escalate an unresolved issue?

Every case has an escalation path published in the support console. A customer can request escalation at any time without justification, which routes the case to a support manager within one hour during covered hours.

Elite customers can escalate directly through their Technical Account Manager, who has standing authority to engage on-call engineering.

## Is phone support available?

Phone support is available on Premium and Elite for Severity 1 and Severity 2 cases, through a dedicated number issued at onboarding. Standard tier support is through the console and email only.

## Where is your support team located?

Support operates from Austin, Dublin, and Singapore, providing follow-the-sun coverage for Premium and Elite. All support engineers are Halebrook employees; no support function is outsourced.

Customers with data residency requirements can request region-restricted support, which limits case handling to engineers located in the customer's home region. This is available on Enterprise and may extend response times outside that region's business hours.

## What training is provided?

Onboarding includes four hours of live administrator training and eight hours of analyst training, delivered remotely. Recorded courses and certification paths are available at no cost through the Halebrook learning portal.

On-site training is available as a professional services engagement.
