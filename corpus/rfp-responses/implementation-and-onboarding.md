# Implementation and Onboarding

Responses drawn from the Meridian Health Network RFP (2025) and the Pemberton Trust RFP (2026). Approved by Professional Services.

## What does a typical implementation look like?

A standard SaaS implementation runs six to eight weeks from contract signature to production operation.

Weeks one and two cover architecture review, network design, and identity integration. Weeks three and four cover sensor deployment and initial discovery. Weeks five and six cover detection tuning and integration with the customer's SIEM and ticketing systems. Weeks seven and eight cover analyst training and transition to steady-state support.

Self-managed implementations typically run ten to fourteen weeks because infrastructure provisioning sits with the customer.

## What resources do you expect from us?

A technical project lead at roughly 50% allocation for the duration, a network engineer for approximately 40 hours concentrated in weeks one through four, and an identity administrator for approximately 16 hours in weeks one and two.

An executive sponsor is expected to attend a kickoff and three checkpoint reviews.

## Is professional services engagement mandatory?

A Launch package is included with every Enterprise subscription and covers the implementation described above. Standard and Professional tier customers can self-implement using published runbooks, or purchase the Launch package separately.

Customers with more than 25,000 assets, more than three deployment regions, or a self-managed deployment are required to engage professional services.

## How do you handle migration from an incumbent tool?

Halebrook provides a migration utility that imports asset inventory and suppression rules from several common platforms, which shortens the tuning phase.

Detection logic is not migrated automatically. Halebrook's detection content is maintained by its own research team, and the implementation includes a mapping exercise to confirm that the customer's existing use cases are covered before the incumbent is decommissioned.

A parallel-run period of four weeks is recommended and is included in the Launch package.

## What does success look like at go-live?

Three criteria are agreed at kickoff and verified at go-live: discovery coverage of at least 95% of known assets reconciled against the customer's CMDB, false positive rate below an agreed threshold on the top ten detection categories, and at least five analysts certified on the platform.

## What happens after go-live?

Standard and Professional customers transition to the support process. Elite customers receive a named Technical Account Manager and a quarterly service review covering adoption metrics, detection efficacy, and roadmap alignment.

A 30-day post-go-live stabilization period is included in the Launch package, during which the implementation team remains engaged for tuning.
